# U.S. annual unemployment rate

The U.S. annual unemployment rate rose by 0.4 percentage points, from 3.6% in 2023 to 4.0% in 2024. The two source rows are [“2023,3.6”](us-unemployment.csv#L2) and [“2024,4.0”](us-unemployment.csv#L3).

The CSV transcribes the unemployment rate columns from the United States row of table 1 in the March 5, 2025 release. The table covers the civilian noninstitutional population aged 16 and over. [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2023–24 annual averages](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/srgune_03052025.htm)
